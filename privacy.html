Privacy Policy

Imminent · Effective November 2, 2026

In short: Imminent collects no data. Everything you enter stays on your own devices, and nothing is sent to us or anyone else.

What we collect

Nothing. Imminent has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. We don't run any servers that receive your information.

Where your countdowns are stored

Your countdowns are saved on your iPhone. If you use the Apple Watch app, your iPhone sends them directly to your paired watch. Private countdowns are never sent to the watch. Your data is included in your device backups like any other app's data, under Apple's terms.

Permissions the app may ask for

  • Calendars — only when you choose Import from Calendar, to list your upcoming events and keep imported countdowns on the right date. Calendar data is read on your device and never leaves it.
  • Face ID — to unlock countdowns you've marked Private. Face ID is handled entirely by iOS; the app never sees your face data.
  • Notifications — to send the reminders you set. They're scheduled on your device.
  • Live Activities — to show a countdown on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.

You can change any of these at any time in the Settings app.

Siri and Shortcuts

When you use Siri with Imminent, Apple processes your request under Apple's own privacy policy. Imminent only receives the text of the countdown you asked to add.

Children

Imminent doesn't collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
