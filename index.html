Scanning pictures and PDFs

What can Scan for Countdowns read?

Anything printed with a date on it: posters and flyers, invitations, bills, class syllabi, game and practice schedules, appointment cards, and screenshots of emails or web pages. Tap + → Scan to Add, then:

  • Take Photo finds the edges of the page and flattens it. You can take several pages, like a long syllabus.
  • Choose Photo reads up to 5 pictures or screenshots from your library.
  • Choose File reads a PDF or picture from Files, iCloud Drive, or other storage apps.

Imminent reads times, the due date on a bill, a practice that repeats until the season ends, and "for 10 days" on a medicine label (as a countdown to the last day). You always check the details before anything is added. Printed text works best; handwriting may not be read.

Are my pictures and files uploaded anywhere?

No. Pictures and files are read right on your device with Apple's built-in text recognition. They're never saved by Imminent, and never sent to us or anyone else. On devices with Apple Intelligence, Apple's on-device model may suggest better names for the events; that happens on your device too.

How do I add a whole schedule at once?

When a page has several dates, Imminent lists them all. Upcoming ones are ticked; past ones aren't. Under Change Selected, use Set Category or Set Emoji to change every ticked event at once. Tap an event to change just that one. Turn on Also Add to Calendar if you want them in Calendar too, then tap Add.

Why did it say "No Upcoming Events"?

The page had dates, but none were things coming up: the date a letter was written or an email was sent, a printed or order date, or a date of birth. Imminent lists each date it left out and why. If one of them is what you wanted, tap Use Anyway. "No Dates Found" means there were no dates at all; try again with the whole page in view, flat, and in good light.

It got a date or name wrong.

Tap the event before adding it to fix the name, date, time, category, or emoji. A sharper, straighter photo helps, and so does Take Photo, which flattens the page. If a page keeps reading wrong, send it to us with ? → Contact Support so we can improve it.

Can I choose a file from Google Drive or OneDrive?

Yes. In Scan for Countdowns, tap Choose File, then Browse at the bottom. If Google Drive or OneDrive isn't listed under Locations, tap ⋯ (top right) → Edit, turn it on, and tap Done. It then shows up whenever you choose a file, in any app.

The Google Drive or OneDrive app needs to be installed and signed in. If it isn't listed under Edit, open that app once, then try again. You can also share a PDF or picture straight from those apps: tap Share (or Open In), then Imminent.

Adding countdowns

How do I add a countdown from a text message?

Copy the message, then in Imminent tap + → New Countdown → Paste. You can also select text in most apps and tap Share → Imminent.

What can I say to Siri?

"Add an event to Imminent" — Siri asks what it is and when, like "Mom's birthday May 10" or "Concert Friday at 8pm". To hear what's next: "What's next in Imminent". For short timers like 20 minutes, use the built-in Clock timer.

How do I add holidays?

Tap + → Add Holidays to add the next year of holidays from your device's holidays calendar. Holidays you already have are skipped, and each one moves to next year's date on its own. If the list is empty, turn on the holidays calendar in the Calendar app.

Calendar

An imported calendar event moved — will my countdown update?

Yes. Countdowns imported from a one-time calendar event follow it if it moves, is renamed, or is deleted. Use Edit in Calendar in the countdown to change the date. Repeating events come in with the same repeat, like every weekday, but stay put if you later change the event in Calendar.

Can Imminent import from my Outlook calendar?

Only if your Outlook calendar is also in Apple's Calendar app. Imminent imports from the calendars in Calendar, like iCloud and Google. The Outlook app keeps its calendar to itself, so Imminent can't see it there.

To add it, go to Settings → Apps → Calendar → Calendar Accounts → Add Account. Choose Microsoft Exchange for a work or school account, or Outlook.com for a personal one. Once its events show in Calendar, use + → Import from Calendar in Imminent.

Many workplaces allow their calendar only in the Outlook app. If adding the account is blocked or asks you to use Outlook, Imminent can't read that calendar. You can still add those events to Imminent yourself.

Repeats and Days Since

How do I make a countdown repeat every weekday, or on the last Friday of the month?

In the countdown, tap Repeats. Every Day, Every Weekday, Every Week, Every 2 Weeks, Every Month, and Every Year are right there. For anything else, choose Custom: pick days of the week, dates of the month (like the 1st and 15th), a position (like the last Friday or the first weekday), or months of the year (like the fourth Thursday of November). Turn on End Repeat to stop on a certain day; after that, the countdown moves to Past.

How do I count up from something, like quitting smoking?

Add a countdown with the start date and turn on Count Up (Days Since), or choose the Milestone category. Imminent lets you know on day 100, 200, 500, and 1,000, and on each anniversary; turn that off with Milestone Alerts.

Widgets, Watch, and Lock Screen

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, and choose Imminent. Countdowns and Favorites come in small, medium, and large sizes (and extra large on iPad). To pick which countdown a widget shows, touch and hold it and tap Edit Widget. For the Lock Screen, touch and hold the Lock Screen and tap Customize.

Why doesn't a countdown show on my watch or widget?

Countdowns marked Private are kept off widgets, the watch, and Siri on purpose. Turn off Private in the countdown to show it everywhere. For the watch, open Imminent on your iPhone once so it can sync.

The Live Activity didn't appear.

Live Activities appear for timed countdowns in their final 8 hours. Check that they're allowed in Settings → Imminent → Live Activities, and that the countdown's Live Activity switch is on.

Devices, sync, and sharing

How do my countdowns get to my other devices?

Through your own iCloud account. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple Account on each device, and that Imminent is allowed to use iCloud in Settings → [your name] → iCloud, under the apps using iCloud. The bottom of the list shows when Imminent last synced, or what's wrong if it can't. Changes can take a minute to appear. Your Apple Watch gets its countdowns from your iPhone.

Does Imminent work on iPad and Mac?

Yes, with the same purchase. On iPad (and on Mac computers with Apple silicon), Imminent shows a sidebar of your categories, your countdowns in a grid, and the one you tap open alongside them. Use the search field to find a countdown by name, and ⌘N to add one with a keyboard. In Split View or Slide Over, it switches to the iPhone layout.

How do I share a countdown with a friend?

Touch and hold a countdown and tap Share. Your friend gets a link that ticks down live, with Add to Calendar. If they have Imminent, the link opens right in the app so they can add it.

I updated, but I don't see the new version.

Open the App Store, tap your picture at the top right, and pull down on that screen to refresh the list of updates. To have apps update on their own, turn on Settings → Apps → App Store → App Updates. The version you have is shown at the bottom of Imminent's list.

Privacy

How do private countdowns unlock?

Tap a locked card or the lock at the top of the list and use Face ID or your passcode. They lock again whenever you leave the app.

What does Imminent collect?

Nothing. There's no account, no ads, and no tracking. Your countdowns stay on your devices and in your own iCloud, and pictures and files you scan are read on your device and never kept. See the Privacy Policy.
